The process

Your shipment, step by step

A clear booking flow, verified handoffs and practical updates from collection to destination.

01
Step 1

Book your slot

Use the route checker on patwadi.com to select your corridor, item type and preferred collection date. Pick your time window — morning, afternoon, evening or night. Pay via Razorpay to confirm your slot.

You will receive a booking confirmation with your unique tracking code.

Route checker on patwadi.com Booking reference UPI, cards and net banking
02
Step 2

Pack your items

Pack securely in a carton, bag or suitcase. Fragile items must be declared at booking and bubble-wrapped before handoff. Label the parcel clearly with the recipient's name, phone number and city. We add a Patwadi tracking label at collection.

Declare fragile items Add recipient details Tracking label at pickup
03
Step 3

We collect and verify

Our last-mile agent arrives within the confirmed collection window. The parcel is logged with a code and collection photo, and you receive confirmation once the handoff is complete.

You → Pickup agent → Line-haul transit → Destination agent → Recipient

Verified at every handoff. Collection and transfer records help Patwadi trace who received the parcel and when each movement occurred.

Collection receipt Verified handoff Custody record
04
Step 4

Track your shipment

Enter your booking reference on the Patwadi tracking page to view the latest recorded shipment milestone. Notifications keep you updated as the parcel moves.

Collected — picked up and logged by the agent
In transit — moving along the confirmed corridor
Arrived — received at the destination handoff point
Milestone tracking Automatic notifications
05
Step 5

Collect at destination

Once your parcel arrives, you receive an arrival notification with the destination agent's contact information and handoff instructions.

Eligible shipments may be held securely for up to seven days. Doorstep delivery is available on selected routes and must be confirmed when booking.

Hold up to 7 days Agent details on arrival Doorstep on selected routes
Route planning

Dispatch times by corridor

These are typical dispatch windows. Final timing depends on the selected route, booking cutoff and confirmed operating capacity.

Delhi, Chandigarh and Punjab corridors Same-day dispatch
Himachal Pradesh — Mandi, Shimla, Manali and Dharamshala Typically within 48hr
Uttarakhand — Dehradun Typically within 48hr
Jammu and Kashmir routes Typically within 72hr
South India — Tamil Nadu, Karnataka, Andhra Pradesh, Telangana and Kerala Typically within 24–48hr

Exact windows are confirmed during booking. Same-day dispatch on plains corridors depends on capacity and the daily booking cutoff.

Frequently asked questions

The following are prohibited:
  • Dangerous goods — flammables, explosives, fireworks and compressed gas
  • Weapons and ammunition — firearms, airguns, prohibited blades and tasers
  • Hazardous chemicals — acids, corrosives, pesticides, bleach and solvents
  • Restricted batteries — including batteries beyond permitted limits
  • Narcotics and controlled substances
  • Improperly sealed liquids
  • Perishable or refrigerated goods
  • Live animals or plants
  • Cash, jewellery and negotiable instruments
  • Original high-risk legal documents such as passports and property deeds
  • Undeclared fragile items
Check the prohibited-items guide or contact Patwadi before booking when uncertain.
