Patwadi vs Safexpress: Which B2B Freight Partner Fits Your Himachal Pradesh Shipments?

Safexpress built the scheduled surface-express backbone for HP’s organised retail and pharma supply chains. Patwadi fills the gap underneath it — for businesses that don’t ship pallets on a schedule but still need GST invoices, tracking, and a reliable corridor.

By the Patwadi Team · Updated 9 June 2026 · 6 min read
Quick answer

Safexpress is built for businesses with consistent, scheduled, pallet-scale volumes on fixed HP corridors — FMCG distributors, pharma companies and large garment manufacturers, typically through contracted 3PL relationships. Patwadi is built for everything underneath that scale: a manufacturer sending a handful of cartons, a retail chain rebalancing stock between two branches, or a hotel restocking from a Delhi supplier — with no minimum order, per-item pricing from Rs 500, GST invoicing, live WhatsApp tracking, and same-rate return shipments.

Where Safexpress fits

Safexpress occupies the “surface express” position — better than a regular courier, cheaper than courier-express, running scheduled services on fixed HP corridors such as Chandigarh–Shimla, Chandigarh–Mandi and Chandigarh–Dharamshala. Its core customers are retail chains, pharma distributors, FMCG companies and electronics brands, plus the garment industry moving goods from Ludhiana into HP.

This works well if your business already has predictable, recurring volume and an existing 3PL relationship to plug into. The scheduling and contract structure is built around that kind of consistency.

Where Patwadi fits

Patwadi is built for the businesses underneath that scale, or whose volume doesn’t follow a fixed schedule:

  • Manufacturers sending sample batches, seasonal inventory, or urgent replenishment on no fixed schedule
  • Retail chains rebalancing stock between their own branches — topping up a slow-moving outlet from a surplus location
  • Hotels and hospitality businesses restocking from Delhi or Punjab suppliers
  • Any B2B shipper who wants GST invoicing and tracking without committing to a 3PL contract or minimum volume
Send one carton or twenty — the price per item is the same.

No volume minimums, no contract, no account setup. Book on WhatsApp, pay UPI, GST invoice on request. Your shipment is tracked the same way whether it is one box or a full manifest.

Can they work together?

For some businesses, the two are not mutually exclusive. Safexpress can handle the predictable, scheduled bulk lane — and Patwadi can take the overflow, the urgent order, or the off-schedule shipment that cannot wait for the next scheduled run, without any penalty for smaller volumes.

Frequently asked questions

Is there a minimum order size for B2B shipments with Patwadi?

No. Patwadi has no minimum order or volume requirement for business shipments. A single carton is priced the same per item as a larger shipment of twenty.

How is Patwadi different from Safexpress?

Safexpress runs scheduled surface-express services for businesses with consistent, pallet-scale volume on fixed Himachal Pradesh corridors, typically through contracted 3PL relationships. Patwadi serves businesses below that scale — no minimum order, flat per-item pricing, WhatsApp booking, and same-rate return shipments.

What is the delivery SLA from Chandigarh or Delhi to Himachal Pradesh?

Patwadi dispatches to Himachal Pradesh and Uttarakhand within 48 hours, compared with 24 hours for Punjab, Delhi and plains routes, and 72 hours for Jammu and Kashmir.

Does Patwadi provide GST invoices for business shipments?

Yes. GST invoices are available on request for all business shipments, with no minimum order required.

Can a small retail business or single store use Patwadi?

Yes. Booking happens directly via WhatsApp with no account setup or contract required, unlike scheduled 3PL services that typically work through contracted accounts and minimum volumes.

Are return shipments charged extra?

No. Return shipments on the same corridor are charged at the same rate as the outbound shipment, in both directions, included as standard.
