Patwadi vs India Post, Delhivery & HRTC Bus Cargo: The Real Way to Send Things to Himachal Pradesh

Four ways to get a parcel, suitcase or carton from Delhi, Chandigarh or Punjab into the hills — compared on cost, speed, tracking and reach.

By the Patwadi Team · Updated 9 June 2026 · 7 min read
Quick answer

For most parcels, luggage and cartons headed to Shimla, Mandi, Manali, Kullu, Dharamshala or Bilaspur, Patwadi is the fastest organised option — 48-hour dispatch, live WhatsApp tracking, and no minimum order. India Post remains the only option for truly remote PIN codes like Lahaul-Spiti and Kinnaur but takes 3–15 days. Delhivery covers only the larger HP towns and pauses during heavy snow. HRTC bus cargo is cheapest per kg but offers no tracking, no guaranteed slot, and no accountability.

India Post / Speed Post — when it is still the right call

India Post’s biggest advantage is one nobody else can match: it is the only service with universal PIN code coverage, including remote areas like Lahaul-Spiti and Kinnaur. It handles items from 0.5 kg to 20 kg and remains the default for documents and consumer-to-consumer parcels.

The trade-off is speed. Delivery can take 3 to 15 days depending on the destination. Use India Post when your destination is a remote PIN code that other services don’t reach, or when you are sending something light where speed genuinely doesn’t matter.

Delhivery, Ecom Express & other e-commerce couriers

Delhivery, Ecom Express and Ekart handle most e-commerce last-mile delivery to Himachal Pradesh’s bigger towns — Shimla, Mandi, Kangra, Solan and Dharamshala. If you are a seller on Amazon or Flipkart shipping to these towns, this is usually the path your order already takes.

Coverage thins fast outside town centres, and these networks are prone to seasonal blackouts during heavy snowfall. Reverse logistics — getting a return shipment back out of HP — is a known weak point across this entire segment.

HRTC bus cargo — the original informal network

Over 3,200 HRTC buses run more than 2,500 routes daily, each with underfloor cargo space. For decades this has been booked the old way — cash to the conductor, around Rs 3–8 per kg, no ticket, no tracking, no SLA, and no recourse if your parcel is left at the wrong stop.

It is genuinely the cheapest way to move bulk goods into the hills. It is also exactly the infrastructure Patwadi formalises. Same buses. Same routes. But booked digitally, with a guaranteed slot and a tracked outcome.

Patwadi — formal booking on the same network

Patwadi runs on North India’s existing intercity bus and freight network and turns it into a service with a guaranteed booking, live tracking, and a paper trail. Booking takes three steps: send your details on WhatsApp, drop the item at a collection point, then follow it live as it moves until it is collected at the other end. Payment is UPI, upfront. GST invoices available on request. Return trips are charged at the same rate.

Frequently asked questions

What is the cheapest way to send a parcel to Himachal Pradesh?

HRTC bus cargo booked informally with a conductor is typically the lowest per-kg cost (around Rs 3–8 per kg) but comes with no tracking and no guaranteed space. Patwadi runs on the same bus network with a flat per-item rate from Rs 500 up to 20 kg and adds tracking, a guaranteed slot, and accountability — making it the cheapest reliable option for most parcels and luggage.

How long does it take to send something from Delhi or Chandigarh to Shimla, Manali, Mandi or Dharamshala?

Patwadi dispatches to Himachal Pradesh destinations within 48 hours. India Post takes 3 to 15 days depending on the destination PIN code. Delhivery typically takes 2 to 5 days but can pause during heavy snowfall.

Does any courier deliver to remote areas like Lahaul-Spiti or Kinnaur?

India Post / Speed Post is currently the only service with universal PIN code coverage reaching remote areas such as Lahaul-Spiti and Kinnaur, though delivery to these areas can take up to 15 days.

Can I track a parcel sent via HRTC bus cargo?

Informal HRTC bus cargo bookings arranged directly with a conductor have no tracking and no digital record. Patwadi formalises the same bus network with live WhatsApp updates at each stage: loaded, in transit, arrived and delivered.

Is there a minimum order size to book with Patwadi?

No. A single carton, suitcase or duffel bag is priced the same per item as a larger shipment of twenty.

Can I send a return shipment back from Himachal Pradesh at the same price?

Yes. Patwadi charges the same rate for return shipments on the same corridor — useful for students sending luggage home at the end of a semester.

Do I need cash to book a shipment with Patwadi?

No. Patwadi is booked over WhatsApp and paid upfront via UPI, with no cash handling at either end. This differs from informal HRTC bus cargo bookings, which are typically cash-only and paid directly to the conductor.
