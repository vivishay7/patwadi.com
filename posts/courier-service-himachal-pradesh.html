How it works

Three steps, no guessing

Route confirmation happens before the parcel is handed over, not after.

1
Send your route on WhatsApp
Share origin, destination, item type, weight and urgency on WhatsApp. Include the receiver’s contact and nearest landmark if the address is not standard.
2
We confirm the route and window
Patwadi checks whether the route is active, the handoff point is clear and the delivery window is realistic. You get a confirmed route plan, not a blind courier promise.
3
Parcel moves with updates
Pickup, movement and handoff details stay in the WhatsApp thread, so the sender and receiver know what is happening and where the parcel is expected.
⚠
What Patwadi does not move: food, perishables, temperature-sensitive items, hazardous materials or items requiring refrigeration. For goods that need cold-chain handling, please use a specialist cold-chain provider.
