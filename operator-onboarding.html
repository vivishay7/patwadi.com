Welcome to the Patwadi pilot network. This packet covers what you need before your first parcel: your role, corridors, custody rules, payouts, prohibited items, and how to report problems.

Full agreement: operator-agreement.html — you accept this in the app before operating.

1. Your role

Patwadi moves parcels between cities on verified private corridors. You join as one of two roles:

RoleWhat you do
LMP (local pickup partner) Customer pickup, handoff to linehaul, receive from linehaul, final delivery to customer — as assigned in the app
Linehaul conductor Create intercity trips, accept parcels at boarding, transport on your corridor, transfer at arrival

You are an independent contractor, not a Patwadi employee. You use your own vehicle or corridor capacity. You are responsible for safe transport and for accidents or third-party claims during your work.

Before you operate: complete KYC on the Patwadi website, wait for admin approval, then accept the operator agreement in the app.

2. Corridors

You may handle parcels only on corridors Patwadi assigns you. Assignments are recorded in the system and may change with reasonable notice.

Pilot focus: Delhi–Chandigarh and connected North India corridors (Shimla, Mandi, Manali, Mumbai–Pune as capacity allows).

If you are unsure whether a parcel is on your corridor, check the app assignment or WhatsApp ops before accepting custody.

3. Custody — the 4-hop chain

Every parcel moves through up to four custody handoffs. At each step you must use the Patwadi app only — verification code + clear photo of the parcel. WhatsApp or verbal confirmations do not count.

StepEventWho acts
1Customer → LMPPickup LMP
2LMP → linehaulPickup LMP at boarding
3Linehaul → LMPDelivery LMP at arrival
4LMP → customerDelivery LMP at dropoff

Your duties while in custody:

  • Keep the parcel secure — never leave it unattended in an open or unsecured place.
  • Hand over only to someone who shows the correct verification code for that step.
  • Never complete a handoff without the physical parcel present.
  • Never reuse an old photo or falsify a record — this is immediate termination.
  • You may refuse fragile items with inadequate packaging; note packaging condition in the app when you accept.

If the app shows you accepted a parcel, you are accountable until the next custody event is recorded. A missing outgoing handoff is treated as a custody failure.

4. Prohibited items — refuse and call us

Do not accept parcels containing:

  • Cash, currency, or negotiable instruments
  • Jewellery, precious metals, or gemstones
  • Alcohol or tobacco
  • Narcotics, controlled substances, or illegal items
  • Weapons, ammunition, or explosives
  • Live animals
  • Perishable food items
  • Hazardous materials (flammable, corrosive, toxic)
  • Items with declared value over ₹10,000 (₹5,000 for electronics)

Full list: Shipping Policy §5. If you suspect a prohibited item, refuse custody and WhatsApp ops immediately. Accepting a known prohibited item is a material breach.

5. Weekly payout

  • Earnings settle every Monday for parcels with a completed delivery (lmp_to_customer) in the prior 7 days.
  • Payment goes to the UPI ID or bank account on your KYC record — keep these accurate.
  • Pilot: no earnings reserve is held.
  • Rates per corridor/leg are communicated by ops at assignment or in your operator rate card.

Deductions: Patwadi may withhold or deduct during customer disputes, lost/damaged parcels in your custody, custody failures, or prohibited-item incidents. Deductions are capped at two settlement periods. You are notified within 24 hours of settlement with the reason.

6. Account and KYC

  • Complete KYC before activation; admin must approve your profile.
  • Your login is personal — do not share credentials or handoff codes.
  • Keep emergency contact and payment details up to date.
  • Patwadi may suspend access for breach, fraud, or failed KYC.
