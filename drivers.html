Three ways to partner

Pick your role

Different partners serve different parts of the corridor. Choose the one that fits what you already do.

Last-Mile
Last-Mile Rider
You handle pickups from senders and deliveries to recipients within a city. Typically 2-wheelers or small vehicles. Ideal for those already operating within one city.
Fleet
Fleet Owner
You provide vehicles and drivers on fixed intercity corridors. Trucks, tempos, or vans — if you run a corridor regularly, you can carry cargo on the return leg or fill gaps in scheduled runs.
Corridor
Corridor Operator
You run a buffer hub or aggregation point between two cities — receiving parcels from one leg and dispatching to the next. Critical for HP hill routes and J&K corridors.
