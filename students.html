What to pack

What to send. What to leave.

Pack smart, avoid surprises at the collection point.

You can send

  • YesClothes and shoes in sealed bags or cartons
  • YesBooks, notes, stationery packed tightly
  • YesBedding, blankets, pillow covers rolled and wrapped
  • YesKitchen items: utensils, small appliances. No liquids.
  • YesElectronics in original or double-boxed packaging
  • YesSports gear and bags within dimension limits
  • YesPosters and frames: well-padded, declared fragile

Do not send

  • NoLiquids: bottles, oils, perfumes, sauces
  • NoPerishables: food, dairy, anything needing refrigeration
  • NoLoose lithium batteries or power banks above 20,000 mAh
  • NoCash, gold, jewellery, or original documents
  • NoGas cylinders or flammables
  • NoFragile items without bubble wrap and declaration
  • NoOversized items without prior WhatsApp confirmation
