Refunds, Cancellations & Claims

When you need to cancel a booking, request a refund, or report a problem with your shipment — this page covers all three.

Effective: 14 June 2026  ·  Patwadi Logistics LLP

Before pickup
Full refund
Cancel any time before the agent arrives. Full refund to your original payment method within 5–7 business days.
After pickup, before dispatch
50% refund
Agent has collected your parcel but it has not left the city yet. Return delivery is arranged. Contact us immediately.
Once in linehaul transit
No cancellation
Parcel is moving between cities. We cannot reverse a linehaul mid-journey. Your parcel continues to destination.

Refunds

How refunds work

1
Contact us with your order ID
WhatsApp or email with your booking reference, the reason for the refund request, and any supporting information. Do this within 48 hours of the event that triggered the request.
2
We verify the custody record
We check handoff timestamps and photos at each custody step. For cancellations this is straightforward. For damage or loss claims, the custody evidence determines where responsibility sits.
3
Decision within 3 business days
We send you a written decision via WhatsApp or email. If a refund is approved, we initiate it immediately.
4
Refund to original payment method
Approved refunds return to the original payment method (UPI, card, or net banking) within 5–7 business days from approval. Exact timeline depends on your bank.

We do not issue refunds in cash. All refunds go to the original payment method only.

Delays & holding

What happens if delivery cannot be completed

If the recipient is unavailable, your delivery partner makes two attempts at no extra charge. The second attempt is within 24 hours of the first.

After two failed attempts, your parcel is held for up to 7 days at ₹100 per day, starting immediately after the second failed attempt. After the 7-day hold, we begin return to sender at the same rate as the original booking.

Tracking exceptions While we investigate a serious delay, your tracking may show “Delivery exception — our team is resolving it.” We will update you by WhatsApp while the investigation is open.
Claims

Damage and lost parcels

48-hour time limit All damage and loss claims must be raised within 48 hours of delivery. For overdue parcels, contact us as soon as the parcel is late. Claims raised after this window cannot be accepted.

Compensation caps

General goods
Up to ₹5,000
Declared value, capped at ₹5,000. Clothing, books, household items, documents, care packages.
Electronics
Up to ₹5,000
Declared value, capped at ₹5,000. Phones, tablets, chargers, accessories.

These caps apply to declared value, not purchase price. Always declare an accurate value at booking — undervalued parcels may receive less compensation. If your parcel is worth more than the cap, contact us before booking.

What to include in a damage or loss claim

1
Your booking reference (order ID)
The reference from your booking confirmation. Format: PW followed by digits.
2
Photos of the damage (damage claims)
Clear photographs of the damaged item and packaging as received. Take these before unpacking fully.
3
Contents description and declared value
Brief description of what was in the parcel and the declared value. A receipt or invoice helps for higher-value items.
4
Description of what went wrong
What you received, how it differed from what was sent, any relevant context.
Exclusions

What we do not cover

  • Items not declared at booking — undeclared fragile items are not covered for damage
  • Items on the prohibited items list — these are not eligible for claims
  • Damage from inadequate packaging you applied before the agent arrived
  • Claims raised more than 48 hours after delivery or the scheduled delivery date
  • Value above the declared caps (₹5,000 for general goods and electronics)
  • Cash, gold, jewellery, or original legal documents — these are prohibited items
  • Consequential or business loss arising from a delay or non-delivery
  • Natural disaster, extreme weather, or force majeure events beyond Patwadi’s control

Need to raise a cancellation or claim?

WhatsApp is the fastest channel. Have your booking reference ready.
