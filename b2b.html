Enterprise corridor logistics

Dedicated planning, written SLAs, and a single point of contact — for organisations that ship intercity on a regular basis.

Talk to our team

What enterprise includes

01

Dedicated corridor planning

We map your routes, lock in dispatch windows that fit your operations, and maintain reserved capacity so your shipments are never displaced by ad-hoc bookings.

  • Origin–destination mapping for all active corridors
  • Fixed dispatch windows aligned to your schedule
  • Reserved capacity on primary routes
  • Route performance reporting monthly
02

SLA-backed dispatch

Agreed timelines per corridor, in writing. Every shipment is tracked at each handoff. If a milestone is missed, your account manager is notified automatically — not you.

  • Written SLA per corridor agreed upfront
  • Milestone tracking: collected → in transit → arrived
  • Automatic escalation on SLA breach
  • Monthly OTIF reporting
03

Key account manager

One person who knows your corridors, your team, and your priorities. Direct WhatsApp access — no ticket queues, no call centres, no re-explaining context every time.

  • Single named contact for your account
  • Direct WhatsApp and phone access
  • Monthly volume and performance review
  • Consolidated monthly billing and reporting
