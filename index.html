The process

5 steps, done

Book above. We handle the rest.

1
Book your slot
Select route, date, and window. Pay to confirm. Booking reference issued instantly.
2
Pack your items
Secure packing. Declare fragile. Label clearly. We add a tracking label at collection.
3
We collect and verify
Agent arrives in your window. Code and photo handoff. Collection receipt issued.
4
Track your shipment
Collected → In Transit → Arrived. Track live on patwadi.com/track.
5
Collect at destination
Agent holds for 48hrs. Collect at your convenience.
