Privacy Policy

Patwadi Logistics LLP  ·  Last updated June 2026

Patwadi Logistics LLP ("Patwadi", "we", "our") operates an intercity parcel delivery network across North, South, and West India, coordinating shipments through verified bus corridor operators. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, share, and protect personal data when you interact with our mobile application, website (patwadi.com), or services. It applies to customers, network operators (Last-Mile Partners), and website visitors.

By registering for or using Patwadi services, you agree to this Policy. This Policy is published in accordance with the Information Technology Act, 2000, the IT (Reasonable Security Practices and Procedures and Sensitive Personal Data or Information) Rules, 2011, and the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (DPDP Act), to the extent applicable and notified.

Who is responsible for your data?

Patwadi Logistics LLP is the Data Fiduciary (controller) for all personal data collected through our services. Our registered office is in Chandigarh, India.

For data processing carried out by our service providers (Supabase, Mapbox, Razorpay), those providers act as Data Processors under contracts that bind them to process data only as instructed by us and to maintain appropriate security standards.

What does this Policy cover?

This Policy covers personal data collected via the Patwadi mobile application, the patwadi.com website, WhatsApp-based booking and support, and any communications exchanged with us. It does not cover third-party websites or services that you may access through links on our platform — those are governed by their own privacy policies.

What are the applicable laws?

This Policy is governed by Indian law, including:

  • The Information Technology Act, 2000
  • IT (Reasonable Security Practices and Procedures and SPDI) Rules, 2011
  • Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023
  • Applicable provisions of the Income Tax Act, 1961 and GST laws governing record retention

We collect only the personal data necessary to provide our logistics services. Below is a description of each category of data, what it contains, and why it is collected.

Account information

When you register or book a shipment, we collect your name, mobile number, and email address. Operators additionally provide government-issued ID details and bank account information for KYC verification and payout processing.

Shipment and booking data

For each booking we collect: pickup and delivery addresses, declared parcel contents and category, weight and dimensions, number of parcels, any special handling instructions, and booking history. This data forms the operational record of your shipment.

Custody event records and handoff photos

Every transfer point in your shipment's journey generates an immutable custody event comprising: a custody confirmation code, handoff timestamp, operator identifier, and up to two proof photographs (pickup confirmation and delivery confirmation). These records are append-only and form the audit trail for your shipment. They cannot be altered after creation.

Location data — operators only

We collect GPS location samples from operators (Last-Mile Partners) during active trip legs for corridor verification, custody confirmation, and safety purposes. Location data collection ceases upon completion of each trip leg. Customers are not tracked continuously at any point. We do not track the movement of buses carrying parcels as Patwadi operates on pre-existing scheduled bus routes.

Payment data

We record Razorpay order IDs, payment reference numbers, transaction amounts, and payment status for billing and dispute records. Card numbers, CVV codes, UPI handles, and bank account details are processed exclusively by Razorpay and are never transmitted to or stored by Patwadi.

Device and usage data

Our mobile application may automatically collect: device model and operating system version, app version, crash reports and error logs, and session event data (screens visited, actions taken). This data is collected to diagnose technical issues and improve application performance. We do not use device data to build advertising profiles or share it with advertisers.

Support and communications data

When you contact us via WhatsApp or email, we retain records of those communications to resolve your query and improve our support processes. If you initiate a WhatsApp conversation via a link in our app or website, the conversation is subject to WhatsApp's own privacy policy in addition to ours.

We use personal data only for the purposes described below. We do not use your data to serve advertising, build behavioural profiles for third parties, or engage in any secondary use not disclosed in this Policy.

Delivery and logistics operations

To book, route, assign, and track parcels across our corridor network; to match shipments to available corridor operators; to generate booking confirmations and delivery receipts; and to calculate pricing based on route, weight, and parcel category.

Custody verification and dispute resolution

To maintain an immutable chain of custody across multiple handoff points; to verify that parcels were transferred and delivered to the correct recipients; to investigate damage claims, mis-delivery reports, and support requests; and to provide audit evidence in disputes. Custody event records are relied upon as the authoritative record of shipment movement.

Safety and fraud prevention

To detect and prevent fraudulent bookings, identity misuse, and abuse of the platform; to verify operator credentials and maintain network integrity; to block accounts that violate our Terms and Conditions; and to comply with our legal obligations to report certain activities to authorities.

Legal and regulatory compliance

To comply with applicable Indian law, including GST record-keeping obligations, income tax requirements, and any lawful orders or directions from government authorities, courts, or regulators. Where we are legally required to retain data beyond the periods described in our Data Retention section, we will do so.

Service improvement and analytics

To analyse aggregate and anonymised operational data (such as corridor performance, delivery success rates, and shipment volumes) to improve our network coverage, routing efficiency, and application features. This analysis uses data that has been stripped of personally identifiable information wherever possible.

Communications and notifications

To send booking confirmations, custody update notifications, delivery confirmations, and support responses via push notification, SMS, or WhatsApp. You may manage notification preferences in the app under Settings. Promotional communications are only sent with your explicit opt-in and can be withdrawn at any time.

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal data to any third party for marketing or commercial purposes. We share data only in the circumstances described below and only to the minimum extent necessary.

Service providers (Data Processors)

We share personal data with the following providers to the extent required to deliver our services. Each provider is contractually bound to use data only as instructed and to maintain appropriate security:

  • Supabase (US) — authentication, database, and file storage. User and parcel data is stored on Supabase infrastructure.
  • Mapbox (US) — address search and geocoding for pickup and delivery locations.
  • Razorpay (India) — payment processing. Razorpay processes payment credentials under its own PCI-DSS compliant environment.
  • Expo / EAS (US) — mobile application build, distribution, and over-the-air updates.
  • WhatsApp / Meta (US) — customer-initiated support conversations via WhatsApp links. We do not use the WhatsApp Business API to initiate messages.
International data transfers

Supabase, Mapbox, Expo/EAS, and WhatsApp/Meta are based in the United States. By using Patwadi services, you acknowledge that your personal data may be transferred to and processed in the United States and other jurisdictions where data protection standards may differ from Indian law. We take contractual and technical measures to ensure such transfers are protected to a standard equivalent to the requirements of Indian data protection law.

Law enforcement and legal process

We may disclose personal data to government authorities, law enforcement agencies, or courts when: (a) required by a lawful court order, summons, or regulatory direction; (b) necessary to protect the rights, property, or safety of Patwadi, our users, or the public; or (c) required by applicable law. We will notify you of any such disclosure to the extent permitted by law.

Business transfers

In the event of a merger, acquisition, restructuring, sale of assets, or change of control affecting Patwadi, personal data held by us may be transferred to the acquiring or surviving entity as part of that transaction. We will notify you of any such transfer and of any material changes to how your data is handled as a result.

Aggregated and anonymised data

We may publish or share aggregated and anonymised data — such as corridor shipment volumes, average delivery times, or network coverage statistics — that cannot reasonably be used to identify any individual. This data may be shared with partners, investors, or in public communications without restriction.

What happens when you delete your account?

Account deletion (via Settings → Delete account) permanently removes your profile, sign-in credentials, and active booking data from our systems. However, transaction records, custody events, and parcel records required for legal compliance under Indian tax law will be retained in anonymised form for the periods described above.

Account deletion is not available while you have a parcel actively in transit.

As a Data Principal under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (DPDP Act), and as a user under the IT Rules, 2011, you have the following rights with respect to your personal data held by Patwadi. To exercise any of these rights, contact our Grievance Officer using the details in the Grievance Officer tab.

Right to access information

You may request confirmation of whether we hold personal data about you, a summary of the categories of data we hold, and the purposes for which that data is processed. We will respond within 30 days of a verified request.

Right to correction and erasure

You may request correction of inaccurate or incomplete personal data. You may also request erasure of data that is no longer necessary for the purpose for which it was collected, subject to our legal retention obligations and our need to maintain records for active service delivery. Custody event records, which are append-only, cannot be deleted as they form the legal audit trail of your shipment.

Right to grievance redressal

You have the right to have privacy-related grievances addressed promptly. Our Grievance Officer will acknowledge your complaint within 48 hours and endeavour to resolve it within 30 days. If you are not satisfied with the resolution, you may approach the Data Protection Board of India once constituted under the DPDP Act.

Right to nominate

Under the DPDP Act, 2023, you may nominate another individual to exercise your data rights in the event of your death or incapacity. To register a nominee, contact our Grievance Officer with the nominee's full name and contact details.

Withdrawal of consent

Where our processing is based on your consent (for example, marketing communications or promotional notifications), you may withdraw consent at any time via app Settings or by contacting us. Withdrawal of consent does not affect the lawfulness of processing carried out before withdrawal, nor does it affect processing carried out for legal compliance or contractual performance.

How to exercise your rights

Email our Grievance Officer at admin@patwadi.com with subject line "Data Rights Request" and include: your registered name and mobile number; the nature of your request (access, correction, erasure, nomination, or withdrawal of consent); and supporting documentation where relevant. We will verify your identity before processing any request.

We use minimal cookies and device-level data collection. We do not use advertising trackers, retargeting pixels, or third-party analytics that build behavioural profiles.

Website cookies (patwadi.com)

Our website uses session cookies that are strictly necessary for basic page functionality (for example, maintaining your login session). We do not use persistent tracking cookies, third-party advertising cookies, or cookies from social media platforms on patwadi.com.

You may configure your browser to block or delete cookies. Blocking strictly necessary cookies may affect the functionality of certain pages.

App diagnostics and crash reporting

The Patwadi mobile application, built on the Expo/React Native platform, may automatically collect crash reports, error logs, and diagnostic data when the application encounters a technical failure. This data is used exclusively to identify and resolve bugs. It does not contain personally identifiable information and is not used for marketing or profiling purposes.

No advertising trackers

We do not use Google Analytics, Meta Pixel, Firebase Analytics, or any similar behavioural analytics or advertising technology on our website or in our mobile application. If this changes in the future, this Policy will be updated at least 7 days before such tools are activated, and registered users will be notified via in-app notice or email.

We implement technical and organisational measures proportionate to the risks associated with our processing activities to protect your personal data against unauthorised access, disclosure, alteration, and destruction.

Technical measures
  • All data is transmitted over HTTPS/TLS encryption between clients and our servers.
  • Database access is controlled by Row-Level Security (RLS) policies — each authenticated user can access only their own records.
  • Custody event records are append-only; no modification is permitted after creation by any user including administrators.
  • Payment credentials are processed exclusively by Razorpay in a PCI-DSS compliant environment; Patwadi never receives or stores card or UPI details.
  • Operator administrative access requires separately credentialed authentication from standard user access.
Organisational measures

Access to production data is restricted to essential personnel on a strict need-to-know basis. Database credentials and API access tokens are not shared externally. Operator KYC documents are handled through a restricted onboarding process accessible only to authorised administrators.

Security incident notification

In the event of a personal data breach that is reasonably likely to result in risk to the rights and freedoms of affected users, we will notify the relevant supervisory authority and affected users within the timeframe required by applicable law, and in any event as promptly as possible. The notification will describe the nature of the breach, the data affected, and the steps we are taking to contain and remedy it.

Limits of our responsibility

No system of data transmission or storage can be guaranteed to be 100% secure. We recommend that you use a strong, unique password for your Patwadi account, do not share your login credentials with anyone, and contact us immediately at admin@patwadi.com if you suspect unauthorised access to your account. We cannot accept responsibility for security incidents arising from actions or omissions on your part.
