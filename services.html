What we deliver

Scheduled intercity shipments for individuals, families, and businesses — across North India and beyond.

🧳

Semester & relocation moves

Moving hostels at semester end, shifting for an internship, or heading home for the holidays — send your bags ahead and travel light. We collect from your hostel or PG, transit via our intercity network, and hold at the destination for you to collect.

Four years of accumulated goods — books, appliances, clothes, equipment. Our graduation pack handles up to 5 items in a single booking at a flat rate. No need to make multiple trips or beg friends for help.

Care packages
